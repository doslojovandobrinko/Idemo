# IDEMO KNOWLEDGE BASE
**Level 7: The Living Reference Library**

---

## 1. Scope & Objective
The **IDEMO Knowledge Base** serves as the living reference library, decision log, and research vault for the entire IDEMO ecosystem.
* It is subordinate to all higher-level governing documents.
* Unlike the rigid, immutable governing charters, the Knowledge Base functions as the evolving institutional memory of IDEMO, continuously compiling and preserving destination datasets, calibration details, technical specifications, and historical context.

---

The live system maintains several highly specific, hyper-linked guides inside the `/docs/` folder:

## 3. Mood Orbit Calibration Methodology

The user's coordinates on the $2D$ SVG canvas are processed locally to calibrate recommendation results based on four primary quadrants:

### Quadrant I: Active / Dense (Top-Right)
* **Energy:** High (Urban, lively, social, clubbing, high-energy dining).
* **Environment:** Dense concrete structures, metropolitan environments, high social activity.

### Quadrant II: Active / Sparse (Top-Left)
* **Energy:** High (Sports, mountain climbing, outdoor exploration, dynamic travel).
* **Environment:** High-nature, open-air, low structural density, expansive environments.

### Quadrant III: Meditative / Sparse (Bottom-Left)
* **Energy:** Low (Stargazing, wellness spas, zen retreats, silent natural trails).
* **Environment:** Extreme nature, wilderness, absolute quiet, minimal structures.

### Quadrant IV: Meditative / Dense (Bottom-Right)
* **Energy:** Low (Quiet libraries, high-end galleries, micro-cafes, premium museums).
* **Environment:** Structural depth, historical enclosures, quiet indoor settings.

## 4. Multi-Language Translation Standards

To support global VIP event visitors, all recommendation titles, descriptions, and location metadata must adhere to the following standards:
1. **Script Integrity:** Maintain perfect Cyrillic and Latin script representations for Serbian, matching current orthographic conventions.
2. **Precision Chinese:** High-end, formal, elegant Chinese characters without region-specific slang.
3. **Professional Spanish, German, and Russian:** Highly localized, fluent translation suites compiled in dedicated language files.
4. **Timeless English:** Neutral, sophisticated English representing elite editorial travel literature.

---

## 5. Evolving Dataset Schemas & Algorithms
* **Schema Mapping:** All curation tables are mapped strictly according to `docs/DataDictionary.md`.
* **Cosine/Euclidean Formulas:** Matching scores are computed locally on the traveler's client device to preserve full privacy.
* **Polar Jittering Algorithm:** Resolves overlapping coordinate plots by dispersing duplicate nodes deterministically on the SVG stage.

---

## 6. Historical Decision Log (Master Decision Records)
Every core architectural shift and calibration modification is recorded inside the permanent **Architecture Decision Log** (`docs/ArchitectureDecisionLog.md`).
* **Zero Undocumented Changes:** Every PR, commit, or code patch must match an entry in the log detailing the context, proposed solution, and approved justification.
* **Preserving Lessons Learned:** Historical failures (such as server-side session tracking attempts) are documented to prevent future architectural regressions.
