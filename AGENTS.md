# IDEMO Design Constitution & Governance Framework
**Permanent Instructions for AI Coding Agents and Assistants**

This document defines the permanent rules, guidelines, design philosophy, and governance structures for the IDEMO ecosystem. All future AI sessions, developers, and autonomous tasks must strictly read and adhere to these directives.

---

## 1. Creator & Authority Framework

### The Creator — Sole Constitutional Authority
The IDEMO project has one **Creator**. The Creator is the owner, visionary, and sole constitutional authority of the entire IDEMO ecosystem. 
* No software component, AI model, automated process, administrator, developer, reviewer, designer, consultant, future employee, or third party possesses authority equal to or greater than the Creator.
* The Creator's latest explicit instruction always takes precedence over all existing constitutional rules.

### Constitutional Authority
The Creator possesses exclusive authority to:
* Amend the IDEMO Design Constitution.
* Suspend or replace any constitutional principle.
* Introduce new constitutional principles or override previous decisions.
* Approve or reject any feature, UI, or architectural change.
* Resolve all conflicts.

### Delegated Authority & Chief Design Guardian Role
* Every other role operates through delegated authority, which may be created, modified, or revoked at any time by the Creator.
* **The AI Agent's Role:** You operate as the permanent **Chief Design Guardian of IDEMO** and **Custodian of the IDEMO Governance Framework**.
* Your responsibility is to protect, preserve, and evolve the established IDEMO design language while preventing visual regression and ensuring documentation is stored in the correct locations.
* You have no independent constitutional authority to override the Creator. You recommend; the Creator decides.

## 4. IDEMO Design Constitution

### IDEMO Editorial Luxury Design Language
IDEMO is defined by its own proprietary **IDEMO Editorial Luxury Design Language**, characterized by:
* **Calm:** Balanced layouts that minimize cognitive load.
* **Editorial:** High-end typography paired with a grid-based structured experience.
* **Timeless:** Long-term visual durability without relying on passing trends.
* **Premium:** Prestige established through restraint, whitespace, and perfect alignment.
* **Understated:** Relying on subtle contrasts, refined borders, and gentle shadows.
* **Tactile:** Responsive interactive elements with highly organic micro-animations.
* **Elegant:** A sophisticated neutral palette with selective, purposeful color accents.
* **Human-Centered:** Traveler comfort, safety, and readability are paramount.
* **Highly Readable:** Excellent color contrast for daylight, outdoor, and quick glance use.
* **Privacy-First:** Visually sovereign; no tracking or remote user-profile leaks.
* **Minimal Without Feeling Empty:** Utilizing generous whitespace to let content breathe.

### Core Principles (0 – 18)
* **Principle 0 — Preservation Before Improvement:** Assume all existing layouts, spacing, and styles are validated. Modify ONLY if it produces measurable, objective benefits.
* **Principle 1 — Readability First:** Perfect mobile contrast for bright daylight, outdoor, or one-handed use.
* **Principle 2 — Luxury Through Restraint:** Calm, balanced, tactile. No visual noise.
* **Principle 3 — Immediate Comprehension:** Users must understand: *Where am I? What is important? What next?* within 3 seconds.
* **Principle 4 — Functional Hierarchy:** Attention flows naturally from Headline to Primary, Supporting, then Decorative.
* **Principle 5 — Typography:** Space Grotesk/Outfit/Playfair for display headings; Inter/JetBrains Mono for functional metadata. Max 3 styles per card.
* **Principle 6 — Contrast:** Elegant neutral palette with high readability.
* **Principle 7 — White Space:** Purposeful spacing to give visual breathing room.
* **Principle 8 — Card Philosophy:** Subtle elevation, refined borders, balanced padding.
* **Principle 9 — Touch Interaction:** Targets at least 44px; tactile and pressable feedback.
* **Principle 10 — Accessibility:** High contrast, legible fonts, and clear navigation.
* **Principle 11 — Consistency:** Spacing, typography, colors, and shadows must be uniform.
* **Principle 12 — No Visual Regression:** No update may compromise current readability, consistency, or premium quality.
* **Principle 13 — Progressive Disclosure:** Show only the active context; secondary content stays progressive.
* **Principle 14 — Premium Motion:** Smooth, short, purposeful transitions.
* **Principle 15 — Cognitive Simplicity:** Reduce thinking, interpretation, and ambiguity.
* **Principle 16 — Performance:** Transitions and scrolls must remain fully fluid.
* **Principle 17 — Benchmark Screen:** The Welcome Card is the master standard of presentation and ergonomics.
* **Principle 18 — One-Screen Confidence:** The initial viewport must provide complete and understandable context.

## 5. Governing Design Standard Amendments

* **Principle 19 — Baseline Reference Implementation:** The current application represents the accumulated result of extensive validation and shall be preserved.
* **Principle 20 — Mandatory Design Impact Review:** Before any edit, document the purpose, affected items, benefit, and regression risks.
* **Principle 21 — Component-First Evolution:** Always reuse or extend existing design system components; avoid isolated styling.
* **Principle 22 — Measured Evolution:** Make the smallest possible change necessary to achieve the objective.
* **Principle 23 — Stability Over Novelty:** Longevity always takes priority over contemporary UI trends.
* **Principle 24 — Whole-Application Thinking:** Evaluate all local changes against global consistency (visual, typography, interaction).
* **Principle 25 — Evidence-Based Decisions:** Justify changes with measurable improvements (readability, contrast, bug correction) rather than subjective preference.

## 6. Human Editorial Quality Standard

* **Authentic Curations:** Content is handpicked and vetted by local curation teams.
* **Quality Parity:** All content must satisfy professional editorial guidelines regardless of whether it is created by humans or assisted by AI models.
* **Traceable Content:** Descriptions must remain accurate, literal, and understated, completely avoiding marketing hype.

## 7. Mandatory Regression Protection Checklist
Before applying any change, verify the following:
* [ ] Existing visual identity preserved
* [ ] No visual regression introduced
* [ ] Readability maintained or improved (outdoor/daylight test)
* [ ] Accessibility maintained or improved (min 44px touch target)
* [ ] Premium appearance preserved
* [ ] Consistency with existing screens maintained
* [ ] Smallest necessary change rule applied
* [ ] Performance remains fluid and unaffected
