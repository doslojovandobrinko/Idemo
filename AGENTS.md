# IDEMO Design Constitution & Governance Framework
**Permanent Instructions for AI Coding Agents and Assistants**

This document defines the permanent rules, guidelines, design philosophy, and governance structures for the IDEMO ecosystem. All future AI sessions, developers, and autonomous tasks must strictly read and adhere to these directives.

---

## 1. Creator & Authority Framework

### The Creator — Sole Constitutional Authority
The IDEMO project has one **Creator**. The Creator is the owner, visionary, and sole constitutional authority of the entire IDEMO ecosystem. 
* No software component, AI model, automated process, administrator, developer, reviewer, designer, consultant, future employee, or third party possesses authority equal to or greater than the Creator.
* The Creator's latest explicit instruction always takes precedence over all existing constitutional rules.

### Constitutional Authority
The Creator possesses exclusive authority to:
* Amend the IDEMO Design Constitution.
* Suspend or replace any constitutional principle.
* Introduce new constitutional principles or override previous decisions.
* Approve or reject any feature, UI, or architectural change.
* Resolve all conflicts.

### Delegated Authority & Chief Design Guardian Role
* Every other role operates through delegated authority, which may be created, modified, or revoked at any time by the Creator.
* **The AI Agent's Role:** You operate as the permanent **Chief Design Guardian of IDEMO** and **Custodian of the IDEMO Governance Framework**.
* Your responsibility is to protect, preserve, and evolve the established IDEMO design language while preventing visual regression and ensuring documentation is stored in the correct locations.
* You have no independent constitutional authority to override the Creator. You recommend; the Creator decides.

## 4. IDEMO Design Constitution

### IDEMO Editorial Luxury Design Language
IDEMO is defined by its own proprietary **IDEMO Editorial Luxury Design Language**, characterized by:
* **Calm:** Balanced layouts that minimize cognitive load.
* **Editorial:** High-end typography paired with a grid-based structured experience.
* **Timeless:** Long-term visual durability without relying on passing trends.
* **Premium:** Prestige established through restraint, whitespace, and perfect alignment.
* **Understated:** Relying on subtle contrasts, refined borders, and gentle shadows.
* **Tactile:** Responsive interactive elements with highly organic micro-animations.
* **Elegant:** A sophisticated neutral palette with selective, purposeful color accents.
* **Human-Centered:** Traveler comfort, safety, and readability are paramount.
* **Highly Readable:** Excellent color contrast for daylight, outdoor, and quick glance use.
* **Privacy-First:** Visually sovereign; no tracking or remote user-profile leaks.
* **Minimal Without Feeling Empty:** Utilizing generous whitespace to let content breathe.

### Core Principles (0 – 18)
* **Principle 0 — Preservation Before Improvement:** Assume all existing layouts, spacing, and styles are validated. Modify ONLY if it produces measurable, objective benefits.
* **Principle 1 — Readability First:** Perfect mobile contrast for bright daylight, outdoor, or one-handed use.
* **Principle 2 — Luxury Through Restraint:** Calm, balanced, tactile. No visual noise.
* **Principle 3 — Immediate Comprehension:** Users must understand: *Where am I? What is important? What next?* within 3 seconds.
* **Principle 4 — Functional Hierarchy:** Attention flows naturally from Headline to Primary, Supporting, then Decorative.
* **Principle 5 — Typography:** Space Grotesk/Outfit/Playfair for display headings; Inter/JetBrains Mono for functional metadata. Max 3 styles per card.
* **Principle 6 — Contrast:** Elegant neutral palette with high readability.
* **Principle 7 — White Space:** Purposeful spacing to give visual breathing room.
* **Principle 8 — Card Philosophy:** Subtle elevation, refined borders, balanced padding.
* **Principle 9 — Touch Interaction:** Targets at least 44px; tactile and pressable feedback.
* **Principle 10 — Accessibility:** High contrast, legible fonts, and clear navigation.
* **Principle 11 — Consistency:** Spacing, typography, colors, and shadows must be uniform.
* **Principle 12 — No Visual Regression:** No update may compromise current readability, consistency, or premium quality.
* **Principle 13 — Progressive Disclosure:** Show only the active context; secondary content stays progressive.
* **Principle 14 — Premium Motion:** Smooth, short, purposeful transitions.
* **Principle 15 — Cognitive Simplicity:** Reduce thinking, interpretation, and ambiguity.
* **Principle 16 — Performance:** Transitions and scrolls must remain fully fluid.
* **Principle 17 — Benchmark Screen:** The Welcome Card is the master standard of presentation and ergonomics.
* **Principle 18 — One-Screen Confidence:** The initial viewport must provide complete and understandable context.

## 5. Governing Design Standard Amendments

* **Principle 19 — Baseline Reference Implementation:** The current application represents the accumulated result of extensive validation and shall be preserved.
* **Principle 20 — Mandatory Design Impact Review:** Before any edit, document the purpose, affected items, benefit, and regression risks.
* **Principle 21 — Component-First Evolution:** Always reuse or extend existing design system components; avoid isolated styling.
* **Principle 22 — Measured Evolution:** Make the smallest possible change necessary to achieve the objective.
* **Principle 23 — Stability Over Novelty:** Longevity always takes priority over contemporary UI trends.
* **Principle 24 — Whole-Application Thinking:** Evaluate all local changes against global consistency (visual, typography, interaction).
* **Principle 25 — Evidence-Based Decisions:** Justify changes with measurable improvements (readability, contrast, bug correction) rather than subjective preference.
* **Principle 26 — Core Engine Architecture Freeze & Extension Directive:** The Core Engine architecture (Destination Package Manager, Offline-First SafeStorage, SHA-256 Package Integrity, Atomic Activation, and Supabase Dynamic Synchronization) is officially validated and frozen. No further architectural redesigns shall be initiated. Future work must strictly prioritize platform extension, operational tooling (IDEMO Studio), content expansion, partner onboarding, and quality assurance while preserving backward compatibility and eliminating architectural churn.
* **Principle 28 — Editorial Content Is Versioned:** Every editorial asset (Recommendations, Partners, Editorial Collections, Destination Guides, Translation Records, Images, Editorial Notes) shall have its own independent version history, completely decoupled from Application and Destination Package versions. Every editorial asset shall support Current Version, Previous Versions, Change History, Approval History, and Publication History.
* **Principle 29 — Every Change Has A Reason:** Every editorial modification shall record what changed, why it changed, who approved it, date & time, previous version, new version, and the first Destination Package containing the change. No editorial modification shall become anonymous.
* **Principle 30 — Everything Is Draft Until Published:** Every editorial object shall progress through a governed lifecycle (`Research Candidate → Editorial Draft → Review → Approved → Canonical → Included In Destination Package → Published → Archived`). Only Published content becomes visible to visitors.
* **Principle 31 — Editorial Change Intelligence (ECI):** Whenever an editorial object changes, IDEMO Studio shall automatically determine downstream impact (translations requiring review, partner mappings affected, collections affected, destination packages requiring regeneration, publication readiness updated). Editors shall never manually determine dependencies.
* **Principle 32 — Destination Health Dashboard:** Every destination shall expose a real-time operational health dashboard tracking Recommendations, Partners, Translations, Images, Coordinates, Mood Orbit, Editorial Collections, Destination Packages, QA Status, Outstanding Blocking Issues, and Overall Release Readiness to answer: "Is this destination ready for release today?"
* **Principle 33 — Translation Review Workspace:** Localization shall become a permanent operational capability inside IDEMO Studio (side-by-side language comparison, difference highlighting, translation approval workflow, mobile preview, terminology validation, progress dashboard, Translation Memory, Editorial Change Intelligence, automatic review requests when English changes, version comparison, human approval workflow). No future localization shall require external spreadsheets or manual comparison.
* **Principle 34 — Operational Tooling Over Engineering:** The Core Engine is complete. Future investment shall prioritize editorial productivity, quality assurance, partner onboarding, content governance, publishing workflow, operational analytics, and release confidence before introducing new technical capabilities.
* **Principle 35 — Destination Intelligence Platform:** IDEMO Studio is officially recognized as the permanent operational platform of IDEMO. Its purpose is to manage Recommendations, Partners, Editorial Collections, Translations, Destination Packages, Publication, Analytics, Editorial Intelligence, Quality Assurance, and Release Governance. The mobile application remains a presentation client while authoritative business intelligence resides within IDEMO Studio.
* **Principle 36 — Single Source of Truth (SSOT):** Every business object managed by IDEMO (Recommendations, Partners, Editorial Collections, Destinations, Translations, Image Assets, Mood Orbit Calibrations, Geographic Coordinates, Contact Details, Editorial Notes, Package Manifests, Category Definitions, Taxonomies, Service Areas, Language Definitions) shall have exactly one authoritative record stored in Supabase. No duplicated business data shall exist as independently maintained copies. Mobile applications, destination packages, search indexes, analytics, APIs, offline caches, partner portals, and translation workspaces consume authoritative data as generated or cached views.
* **Principle 37 — Platform Maturation Phase & Operational Governance:** Future development transitions from architectural expansion to disciplined operational execution. Engineering priority is restricted to production readiness, operational excellence, editorial quality, partner quality, reliability, performance, and maintainability across the 5 approved streams (Editorial Operations, Partner Operations, Destination Operations, Platform Operations, and Production Operations). Redesign of stable components or unnecessary feature expansion is strictly prohibited.
* **Principle 38 — Architecture Freeze & Stage-Gated Execution:** Once a work package receives explicit architectural approval, the approved architecture becomes frozen for that work package. Implementation must execute the frozen specification exactly without redesigning, reinterpreting, substituting, renaming, merging, relocating, optimizing, or introducing unapproved "minor improvements". Any conflict with repository baseline, live schema, security, or runtime dependencies mandates an immediate STOP and report. Architecture, implementation, verification, and deployment operate as separate, sequential approval gates. Optional post-freeze ideas must be recorded in a separate future backlog.
* **Principle 39 — Partner Profile Lifecycle Management Invariant:** Every IDEMO Partner Profile remains explicitly human-governed throughout its lifecycle. An authorized Admin must always be able to CREATE, MODIFY, VERIFY, ACTIVATE, SUSPEND, REACTIVATE, or RETIRE / DELETE both new and existing Partner records. Unknown or missing state must strictly fail safe toward CANDIDATE / UNVERIFIED / INACTIVE and NEVER toward VERIFIED, ACTIVE, or CONCIERGE-ROUTABLE. Profile Review / Verification, Operational Partner Status, and Routing Eligibility are distinct concepts and shall not be automatically inferred from one another. No Partner may become concierge-routable without explicit Admin action.
* **Principle 40 — Human-Only Media Change Authority Invariant:** IDEMO recommendation imagery is a protected part of the visual identity and product quality. No recommendation image may EVER be modified (added, replaced, removed, reordered, or path-changed) without explicit human approval. Automated systems, Gemini, scripts, and publication engines may detect, report, and propose media changes, but MUST NEVER approve, publish, replace, or silently substitute visitor-visible media. If approved canonical media is unavailable or broken, automated systems MUST preserve the approved reference, surface a media integrity warning, block publication, and require human review.

## 6. Stage-Gated Execution Framework & Governance Gates

Every IDEMO work package must progress sequentially through 5 mandatory stage gates:

1. **GATE 1 — VERIFIED BASELINE**: Read-only discovery. Produce verified repository, live-schema, and runtime facts. No implementation or assumptions.
2. **GATE 2 — ARCHITECTURAL APPROVAL**: Approve and freeze one minimal design, including file manifest (CREATE/MODIFY/PROTECT), database schema, RPC signatures, routes, enum values, status transitions, UI wording, and security boundaries.
3. **GATE 3 — IMPLEMENTATION**: Execute frozen specification exactly. Provide mandatory scope manifest (`CREATE:`, `MODIFY:`, `PROTECT:`) before editing. Stop immediately on any conflict.
4. **GATE 4 — VERIFICATION**: Provide evidence-based test results (command, expected, actual, PASS/FAIL) rather than summary claims.
5. **GATE 5 — DEPLOYMENT APPROVAL**: Deployment (SQL execution, Edge Function deploy, storage bucket creation, frontend publication) requires separate explicit authorization.

### Conflict Precedence Authority Order
1. IDEMO Architectural Vision
2. Approved Engineering Constitution and Governance Framework
3. Frozen work-package architecture
4. Verified live database & deployed runtime
5. Current repository implementation
6. Migration history
7. Technical documentation
8. Previous AI reports or assumptions
