# IDEMO Engineering Office — Canonical Baseline v1.0

**Document Status:** APPROVED & CANONICAL  
**Authority:** IDEMO Engineering Office  
**Effective Date:** 1 August 2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Scope:** Universal IDEMO Platform Architecture, Data Models & Editorial Knowledge Base  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Governance Intent

The IDEMO Engineering Office hereby formally freezes and establishes **Canonical Baseline v1.0** as the permanent architectural reference and operational standard for the IDEMO ecosystem. 

This baseline encapsulates the completed Wave 1 Editorial Collections architecture, the Wave 2 Catalogue Expansion (152 verified canonical recommendations), the Global Expertise Taxonomy, and the Intelligent Partner Matching Engine. 

From this point forward, all future engineering, editorial, and partner integration activities across all destinations shall treat this baseline as the authoritative reference point.

---

## 2. Architectural Independence Framework

To ensure long-term stability and platform longevity, the IDEMO Canonical Baseline v1.0 strictly distinguishes between four structural layers:

1. **Core Architecture (Canonical):** The structural paradigms, data flow patterns, single global application topology, offline-first engine, and backend-driven state mechanisms.
2. **Data Models (Canonical):** The normalized schemas governing Recommendations, Editorial Collections, Partners, Global Expertise Taxonomy, Visitor Interests, Partner Capabilities, Certifications, Languages, and Service Areas.
3. **Editorial Knowledge Base (Canonical):** The curated recommendation dataset (152 items), editorial collections, spatial coordinates, Mood Orbit positions, and historical/cultural copy standards.
4. **Implementation Technologies (Replaceable):** The specific runtime scripts, bundlers, frontend view libraries, database providers, or UI components used to render the application.

> **Key Directive:** Implementation technologies are replaceable runtime details. They may evolve over time without altering the governing architectural principles or canonical data contracts defined in this baseline.

---

## 3. Frozen Architectural Assets

The following core assets are architecturally frozen and protected under Canonical Baseline v1.0:

### 3.1 Platform Architecture
* **Single Global Codebase Architecture:** One reusable IDEMO application built on a shared core architecture. Implementation technologies may evolve over time without changing the governing architectural principles.
* **Backend-Driven Evolution:** Authoritative business logic, collection mappings, partner matching scores, and privacy boundaries remain managed server-side.
* **Offline-First Capability:** Full client-side availability for recommendations, offline spatial mapping, and resilient client caching without sacrificing cloud synchronization.
* **Mood Orbit 2D Spatial Engine:** Continuous 2D spatial positioning model mapping recommendations across Energy, Social, Luxury, Urbanity, Nature, and Weather dimensions.

### 3.2 Global Expertise Taxonomy & Intelligent Matching
* **Domain & Category Taxonomy:** Standardized hierarchy separating professional expertise from visitor preferences.
* **Dedicated Sub-Models:** Independent models for Visitor Interests, Partner Capabilities, Partner Languages, Service Areas, and Professional Certifications.
* **Multi-Factor Scoring Engine:** Backend-driven partner matching engine incorporating expertise alignment, verification status, language fluency, geographic boundary coverage, availability, and operational performance.

### 3.3 Canonical Editorial Knowledge Base
* **152 Verified Recommendations:** Comprehensive coverage across Nature, History, Gastronomy, Travel, Wellbeing, Medical, and Cultural domains in Serbia.
* **Wave 1 Editorial Collections:** Thematic journeys including *Twentieth Century Global Diplomacy*, *Non-Aligned Movement*, *Three Faiths*, *Roman Emperors' Serbia*, and *Hidden Belgrade*.
* **Editorial Quality Standard:** Neutral, timeless, historically accurate copy written in an understated editorial tone without promotional marketing language.

## 4. Change Control & Governance Policy

Any future proposal seeking to modify the canonical architecture, data schemas, or frozen baseline assets must adhere to the formal **Engineering Office Change Control Process**:

1. **Business Justification:** Clear operational or product necessity that cannot be satisfied by extending existing capabilities.
2. **Architectural Rationale:** Technical explanation demonstrating alignment with IDEMO Governing Principles.
3. **Impact & Regression Assessment:** Comprehensive analysis verifying zero visual, functional, or data-contract regressions.
4. **Migration Strategy:** Deterministic script and schema migration plan preserving backward compatibility.
5. **Formal Approval:** Explicit approval from the Engineering Office prior to any code or schema modification.

---

## 5. Future Platform Evolution Strategy

Future platform expansion shall focus strictly on **extending** the baseline rather than redesigning it:

* **Catalogue Growth:** Introducing new canonical recommendations across unrepresented regions and themes.
* **Editorial Expansion:** Crafting new Editorial Collections from existing and expanded recommendations.
* **Partner Network:** Onboarding, verifying, and routing accredited local partners through the Intelligent Matching Engine.
* **Refinement:** Continuous enhancements to photography, multi-language translations, performance optimization, and UI micro-interactions.

---

## 6. Closing Governance Statement

> **The Canonical Baseline v1.0 is a living baseline, not a frozen product. It shall evolve through controlled, reviewed and approved extensions while preserving the architectural principles, editorial integrity and engineering discipline established by this baseline.**
